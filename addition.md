The addition of 2 integers work by combining the similar places. For example 1 + 1 = 2 because the 1s are in the same place.

Another Example of the addition of 2 integers is 1 + 10. 1 and 0 share the same place so it'll be 1 in the place of zero and nothing replaces the 1 in 10 so the answer is 11.
